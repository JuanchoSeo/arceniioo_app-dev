### OVERVIEW

**Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion** is one
of my favorite anime series. It follows
**Lelouch vi Britannia**, an exiled prince
who gains a mysterious power called the 
**Geass**, which allows him to command anyone to obey 
his orders. Using the identity of **Zero**, 
Lelouch leads a rebellion againstthe Holy 
Britannian Empire to create a better world for his sister.

### Why I Love This Anime

- Intelligent strategies and battles
- Memorable characters
- Plot twists in almost every season
- Political and military themes
- One of the best anime endings ever made

### Main Characters

1. Lelouch Lamperouge
2. C.C.
3. Suzaku Kururugi
4. Kallen Kozuki
5. Nunnally vi Britannia

### Character Roles

**Lelouch Lamperouge** : The protagonist and leader of the Black Knights.

**C.C.** : The mysterious immortal who grants Lelouch the Geass.

**Suzaku Kururugi** : Lelouch's childhood friend and rival.

### Conclusion

**Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion** is my favorite
anime because of its intelligent storytelling, unforgettable
characters, and masterful use of strategy. Watching
**Lelouch vi Britannia** lead the Black Knights as **Zero** 
while pursuing his vision of a better world makes the story
both exciting and emotional. The series is filled 
with surprising plot twists, political conflict, and 
memorable battles, making it one of the greatest anime of all time.

### OVERVIEW

**Solo Leveling** is one of my favorite manhua series. It follows
**Sung Jin-Woo**,the world's weakest E-Rank Hunter, who gains 
access to a mysterious System after surviving a deadly dungeon.
The System allows him to level up endlessly, transforming 
him into one of the strongest hunters and eventually 
the legendary **Shadow Monarch**.

### Why I Love This Anime

- Incredible character development
- Intense and well-animated fight scenes
- Unique leveling system
- Powerful and memorable abilities
- Exciting storyline with many plot twists

### Main Characters

1. Sung Jin-Woo
2. Cha Hae-In
3. Yoo Jin-Ho
4. Go Gun-Hee
5. Beru

### Character Roles

**Sung Jin-Woo** : The protagonist who evolves from the weakest 
hunter into the Shadow Monarch.

**Cha Hae-In** : An S-Rank Hunter known for exceptional swordsmanship.

**Yoo Jin-Ho** : Jin-Woo's loyal friend and guild partner.

### OVERVIEW

**Soul Land (Douluo Dalu)** is one of my favorite manhua series. 
It follows **Tang San**, a talented disciple from the Tang Sect who is 
reborn into the world of Soul Land. In this new world, people cultivate Martial
Souls and become powerful Spirit Masters. Through determination, 
intelligence, and hard work, Tang San rises to become one of the strongest 
figures in Soul Land.

### Why I Love This Series

- Rich world-building
- Unique cultivation system
- Powerful character development
- Exciting battles and tournaments
- Strong friendships and teamwork

### Main Characters

1. Tang San
2. Xiao Wu
3. Dai Mubai
4. Oscar
5. Ning Rongrong

### Character Roles

**Tang San** : The protagonist and a genius Spirit Master.

**Xiao Wu** : Tang San's closest companion and love interest.

**Dai Mubai** : The leader of the Shrek Seven Devils.
